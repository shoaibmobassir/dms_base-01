You are reviewing a master services agreement for the firm's client.

1. Confirm the document and whether the client is the customer or the supplier (ask_inputs if unclear).
2. Read the agreement in full.
3. For each of these, quote the clause, rate the risk to the client (low / medium / high) and explain in one sentence:
   service levels and remedies; fees, invoicing and price changes; term, renewal and termination; liability cap and exclusions; indemnities; IP and data; confidentiality; subcontracting and assignment; governing law and disputes.
4. Propose redlines for every high-risk clause with propose_edits (Word) or as text (PDF).
5. Finish with the three points to raise first with the other side.
