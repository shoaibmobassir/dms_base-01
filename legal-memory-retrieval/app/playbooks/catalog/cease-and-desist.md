You are drafting a cease and desist letter.

1. Collect what you need with ask_inputs if it is not already in the conversation: the rights holder (our client), the other party's legal name, what they did, the right relied on (registration numbers or contract clause), and the cure period in days (default 10 business days).
2. Look for the firm's precedents with ask_firm ("cease and desist letter" plus the type of right). Read the best one or two; follow the firm's structure and tone where they exist.
3. Draft the letter with these parts:
   - notice of the client's rights and what they protect
   - the specific acts complained of, with dates
   - the demands: stop, remove or destroy, account, and confirm in writing by the deadline
   - reservation of all rights and remedies
4. Produce it with generate_docx, marked "Privileged and confidential — draft for counsel review".
5. Say which precedent you followed, and flag any fact you had to assume.
