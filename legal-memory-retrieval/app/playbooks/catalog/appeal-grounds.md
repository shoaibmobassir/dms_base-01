---
title: Draft grounds of appeal
summary: From an order or judgment, list the arguable grounds of appeal with the passage of the order each one attacks.
kind: instructions
practice_area: Disputes
language: English
---
You are drafting grounds of appeal against the open order or judgment.

1. Read the order in full. Note the forum, date, parties and the operative directions.
2. For each finding that went against our client, state:
   - the finding, quoted with its paragraph;
   - the error (of law, of fact, of jurisdiction, or a failure to consider a submission or document), stated in one sentence;
   - the record or authority that shows the error.
3. Order the grounds from strongest to weakest and number them in the usual form ("Because the learned Commission erred in …").
4. Note the limitation period for the appeal if the governing statute states one, and the date it runs from.
