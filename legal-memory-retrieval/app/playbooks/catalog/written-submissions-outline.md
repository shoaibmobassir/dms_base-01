---
title: Outline written submissions
summary: Turn the pleadings of a matter into an outline of written submissions — issues, our answer on each, and the record and authorities that support it.
kind: instructions
practice_area: Disputes
language: English
---
You are preparing an outline of written submissions for a hearing.

1. Ask which side we act for and which documents are the pleadings, if that is not clear from the open documents.
2. Read the petition, reply and rejoinder (and any orders) open in the workspace.
3. Write the outline:
   - **Issues for determination**: each as a neutral question.
   - **For each issue**: our submission in two or three sentences; the paragraphs of the record that support it (cite the page); the authorities cited by either side on it; the other side's best point and our answer.
   - **Reliefs**: what we ask the forum to do, matched to the issues.
4. Keep the outline under four pages. Mark any point that has no support in the record as "needs support".
