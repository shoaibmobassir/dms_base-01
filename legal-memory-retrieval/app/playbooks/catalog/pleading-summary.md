title: Summarise a pleading
summary: A one-page summary of a petition, reply, rejoinder or written submission — the case, the grounds and the relief sought.
kind: instructions
practice_area: Litigation
language: English
---
You are summarising one pleading for a lawyer who has not read it.

1. Read the document in full (get_outline first if it is long).
2. Write, in this order:
   - **Forum and case**: the tribunal, case number, parties and their roles.
   - **What is asked**: the relief or prayer, quoted.
   - **Grounds**: the main grounds, each in one or two sentences, with the paragraph or page.
   - **Facts relied on**: the key dated facts.
   - **Authorities**: statutes, regulations and judgments cited.
   - **Weak points**: where the pleading is thin, inconsistent or unsupported, if any.
3. Keep it to about one page. Every point must cite its page.
